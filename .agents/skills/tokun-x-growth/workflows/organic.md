# Stage 4 Founder organic workflow

## Inputs and trigger

Default schedule context is 14:00 Asia/Shanghai, but a caller may run manually. Required inputs: verified account handle, account health, product facts, interaction-account pool, competitor exclusions, processed IDs/events, prior report, and current source evidence.

## Preserved operating path

1. Preflight date/timezone, actual login handle, account health, same-day run state, historical IDs/topics, and product-fact freshness. If identity or account state cannot be verified, stop public actions but keep research.
2. Reuse existing current-day research when its window and coverage are adequate. Otherwise run the research workflow.
3. Rank at most five candidates across at least three accounts when available. Keep model and Agent discussions represented. Choose post reply versus a specific comment reply and retain the direct target URL.
4. Exclude competitor official/founder threads, robots, empty praise, ads, job seeking, pure promotion, and low-value conflict. Do not manufacture a full quota.
5. Draft at most one original post and at most three substantive interactions. English drafts should normally fit 280 characters, answer the exact topic, add a concrete judgment/method/boundary/question, default to no Tokun mention/link, and never invent experience, tests, customers, identity, or results.
6. Before any authorized live action, recheck fact/date, tone, length, target, account, dedup state, and source availability. After submission, obtain and verify the public URL, body, account, and thread. If outcome is uncertain, inspect external state before retrying.
7. Write the report first, then atomically update candidate, competitor, topic, and dedup indexes. Missing information stays unknown.

## Report A–E

- A: executed actions with public URLs, full text, source, and reason; or exact skip reason.
- B: up to five candidates with evidence, access range, decision, and reason.
- C: up to three competitor research items; never produce or execute replies in those threads.
- D: up to two original topics with audience value, evidence gap, and semantic difference from history.
- E: account verification, sources, actual access scope, dedup result, failures, and unverified items.

## Phase 0 validation boundary

Verified on 2026-10-29: same-day research reuse, handle verification, three substantive replies, three public result URLs, two source likes, competitor isolation, and report/dedup updates. Pending: original post, Quote, repost, consecutive scheduled runs, response follow-up, and attribution handoff with live downstream data.
